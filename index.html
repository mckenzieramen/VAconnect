ABOUT VA CONNECT

A smarter way to navigate
the VA job market.

VA Connect turns the Global VA Agencies directory into a practical job-search workspace. Instead of repeatedly opening a spreadsheet, users can visually browse opportunities, check eligibility notes, visit the source website, and keep application progress in one place.
